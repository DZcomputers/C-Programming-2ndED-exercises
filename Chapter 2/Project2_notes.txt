EXERCISE 2 - AUTHOR'S NOTES (25/07/2026).

  	When entering the fraction as 4.0f / 3.0f, the result - fixed to 1 decimal - is 4186.7. But when it's written as just 4 / 3, it's just 3140.0.
	I did the math and the result with the floating fraction is the actual one.

	Let A be some rational number:
	A * 3.14 * 10³ = 3140.0 
	A * 3.14 * 1000 = 3140.0
	A * 3140 = 3140.0
	A = 1
	4/3 != 1, it's 1.[3] <- the square brackets means the number repeats endlessly.

	C is known for being picky about its data types, but I didn't know it was picky to the point where operations that had integers in them HAD to result in an integer - same thing for floats.
